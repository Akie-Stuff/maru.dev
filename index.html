// About Me

I operate across the convergence of low-level kernel architectures, autonomous robotics software, and high-performance computing. As an active Open-Source Contributor to Linux Kernel subsystems and Student Researcher in FPGA & vDPA acceleration, I design deterministic embedded systems while exploring classical logic systems and cognitive science.

Beyond assembly instructions, kernel patches, and robotic kinematics, I manifest thoughts into words and sound—writing technical essays, crafting lyrics, and composing instrumental pieces that capture emotional resonances traditional language often fails to articulate.
